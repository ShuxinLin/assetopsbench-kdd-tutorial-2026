Building Reliable Industrial Agents with MCP:
A Hands-on AssetOpsBench Tutorial for AI-Driven Operations

2026 KDD Hands-on Tutorial

Tuesday, August 11, 2026   1:30 PM – 4:30 PM

Location: Jeju, Korea

Abstract

Industrial asset operations and maintenance require decisions grounded in heterogeneous data, including sensor streams, asset hierarchies, failure-mode catalogs, and work-order histories. Large language model agents can unify these sources and automate workflows such as condition monitoring, fault diagnosis, and maintenance planning, but deployment in safety-critical environments demands reliable tool use, traceable reasoning, and rigorous evaluation. This tutorial introduces the Model Context Protocol (MCP) as a practical foundation for connecting agents to industrial systems and AssetOpsBench as a reproducible environment for developing and benchmarking them.

Participants will progress from core MCP concepts to hands-on execution with tools for utilities, IoT and asset registries, failure-mode retrieval, work orders, time-series analysis, and vibration diagnostics. Using guided notebooks, they will build a grounded work-order agent with Stirrup, inspect its tool-use trajectory, evaluate its outputs against static scenario data, and compare systems on an auditable leaderboard. The tutorial connects architectural choices with grounding, safety, provenance, robustness, and operational cost, equipping researchers and practitioners to design, diagnose, and evaluate reliable AI agents for real-world industrial operations.

Schedule

  • Section 1: Foundations and Environment Check (1:30–1:55 PM, 25 min)
    - Confirm Docker, CouchDB, the notebook kernel, and readiness checks
    - Introduce MCP tools, clients, servers, and agent workflows
    - Explore the AssetOpsBench architecture and benchmark pipeline
  • Section 2: Industrial MCP Server Tour (1:55–2:40 PM, 45 min)
    - Discover and call the Utilities, IoT, and asset-registry tools
    - Retrieve failure modes and analyze work orders through MCP
    - Profile time-series evidence and explore vibration diagnostics
  • Section 3: End-to-End Grounded Work-Order Agent (2:40–3:25 PM, 45 min)
    - Configure Stirrup and build a grounded work-order question
    - Run the agent and persist its tool-use trajectory
    - Audit the result for grounding, safety, and output quality
  • Break (3:25–3:35 PM, 10 min)
  • Section 4: Evaluation and Leaderboard (3:35–4:05 PM, 30 min)
    - Evaluate a Stirrup trajectory with the static-JSON evaluator
    - Report pass rate and exact match across separate execution tracks
    - Rank results and export an auditable leaderboard
  • Section 5: Advanced Exercise and Open Challenges (4:05–4:20 PM, 15 min)
    - Stage an FCC scenario, verify the record through MCP, and classify a failure code
    - Discuss grounding, provenance, robustness, and production deployment
  • Q&A (4:20–4:30 PM, 10 min)
